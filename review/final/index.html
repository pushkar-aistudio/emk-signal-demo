What changed in this QC pass

Bug 1 — silent dead-air pauses before every flash-cut. The original assembly padded whichever of a beat's video/audio was shorter up to match the longer one. For six of the ten rows (1, 3, 4, 5, 8, 9) the silent video clip ran longer than its own narration, so the assembly stretched the audio with trailing silence to match — beat 5 alone got 3.35s of dead silence tacked onto a 2.81s line. That silent hold, right before every white-flash cut, is what read as "the transition pauses and the video feels disconnected." Fixed in scripts/assemble-final.sh: each segment's length is now always the beat's own narration length (durA) — "the video follows the voice," this project's own stated convention — so a clip longer than its narration is trimmed to the narration's end instead of stretching the audio with silence. A clip shorter than its narration still freezes its last frame (beats 2, 4b, 6 — nothing else to show).

Bug 2 — a torn/corrupted frame right after the beat 3→4 cut. Frame-accurate extraction found a genuinely corrupted frame (top-left ~47% showing real content, the rest flat grey) landing exactly on the first visible frame after the flash into beat 4. Traced to its source: beat4/clip.mp4's own frame 0 is that corrupted frame (confirmed in the raw take itself, before assembly ever touches it — frame 1, 40ms later, is clean). The source clip wasn't touched; the assembly now starts reading that one input 40ms in (the same front-of-take-preamble trim this project already uses elsewhere), so the corrupted frame is never selected.

Also picked up beat 7's real re-time (1ec1ea5/23eead7, landed on this same repo mid-QC) — its old 10.00s clip and 3.8s freeze-pad are gone; the new 14.56s clip trims cleanly to its 13.82s audio like every other beat now.

What is NOT an assembly bug (reported, not fixed here)

  • Beat 3's three source-snippet cards land visibly ~1.2s after their respective cue words ("which accounts" / "who" / "what to say"), consistently — verified identical in the isolated beat3/clip.mp4, so it's baked into that beat's own choreography, not introduced by muxing.
  • Beat 1's "multiplying the work" cue (6.34s into its own clip) lands while the account-grid visual is already well into its own fade-to-white — could be intentional (fading out to represent scale), flagging since it's ambiguous rather than asserting it's wrong.
